The Right Teacher Changes Everything

From jazz and classical to rock, folk, flamenco, and beyond — our team covers every genre and every skill level. We never guess: we listen to your goals and match you intentionally.

Jazz Guitar Music Theory
Freddy Ezeala
Lead Instructor

Freddy brings deep jazz vocabulary and music theory expertise to every lesson. Whether you're exploring jazz harmony for the first time or working through advanced chord voicings, Freddy guides with patience and precision. Ideal for students who want to understand not just how to play, but why it works.

Book with Freddy
Electric Guitar Ukulele
Houston Rehmet
Instructor

Houston teaches both electric guitar and ukulele, making him one of our most versatile instructors. Energetic and encouraging, he's especially great with students who want to play their favorite songs as quickly as possible. His upbeat teaching style keeps students engaged and coming back.

Book with Houston
Guitar
Scott Montgomery
Instructor

Scott's straightforward, no-nonsense approach resonates with students who want to get right to playing. His focus on strong fundamentals ensures every student builds a foundation they'll rely on for life. A great fit for self-motivated learners of any age who appreciate direct, clear instruction.

Book with Scott
Bass Upright Bass Jazz Theory
Cade Meaderis
Instructor

One of the very few instructors in the Plano area who teaches both electric bass and upright bass alongside jazz theory. Cade is the go-to teacher for students serious about becoming complete, well-rounded musicians. He's equally at home teaching slap bass, walking bass lines, and classical upright technique.

Book with Cade
Acoustic Guitar Classical Flamenco
Ryan Hughes
Instructor · 10+ Years at TML

Ryan covers it all — acoustic, electric, classical, and flamenco guitar across pop, rock, and folk styles. With over 10 years teaching at Texas Music Lab, he's one of our most trusted and beloved instructors. Outside the studio, Ryan is an avid cyclist, cook, and woodworker who brings the same patience and craft to his teaching.
