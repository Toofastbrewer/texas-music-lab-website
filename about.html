Who We Are

Built on a Belief That
Music Belongs to Everyone

Texas Music Lab was founded on a simple idea: every person who walks through our door deserves access to great music education, quality instruments, and honest repairs — regardless of their experience level or budget.

Over the years, we've built a team of working musicians who also happen to be exceptional teachers. We've curated an instrument inventory worth playing. We've developed a reputation for repairs done right the first time. And we've become a place where Plano's music community comes together.

Located at 2229 W 15th St, Suite B, we serve students from Plano, Allen, Frisco, McKinney, and across the DFW metroplex. Whether you're 7 or 70, a first-timer or a lifelong player, there's a place for you here.

What We Stand For

Our Values

These aren't buzzwords — they're how we make every decision at Texas Music Lab.

Intentional Matching

We listen before we match. Every student gets the instructor who genuinely fits their goals — not whoever has an open slot.

Honest Always

Whether it's a repair quote or an instrument recommendation, we tell you the truth — even when it costs us a sale.

Music for Everyone

Age, background, and skill level don't matter here. If you want to learn, we want to teach you. No barriers, no judgment.

Community First

We're part of Plano's fabric. Supporting us means supporting local music education, local musicians, and the local community.

In Memoriam

"Let us share our passion for music with you."

Jack Buras — Founder, Texas Music Lab
Passed away October 2025
Jack founded Texas Music Lab with a belief that music education should be accessible, personal, and joyful. He built a team that shared that vision, and that team carries it forward every day. His legacy lives on in every student who walks through our doors and every lesson we teach.
