FAQs

Service Questions

How do I know which lesson length is right for me?
+
For children under 10 or complete beginners, 30-minute lessons are often ideal. For intermediate players or students with specific goals, 60-minute lessons allow time for technique, theory, and repertoire. When in doubt, call us and we'll help you decide.
What's included in a full guitar setup?
+
A full setup includes action adjustment at the nut and saddle, truss rod inspection and adjustment, intonation tuning, fret polish, and a restring. We'll also flag any other issues we notice and quote them separately before touching anything.
Do you buy used instruments?
+
We occasionally purchase used instruments depending on inventory. Bring it in and we'll take a look. We're always honest about what we can offer and will give you a fair assessment of its market value.
Can I switch instructors if it's not a great fit?
+
Yes, absolutely. We want the match to be right. If your first instructor isn't clicking, just let us know and we'll move you to someone who fits better — no awkwardness, no penalty.
