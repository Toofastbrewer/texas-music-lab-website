1. Privacy Policy

Texas Music Lab ("we", "us", "our") operates this website and provides music lessons, instrument sales and instrument repair services from 2229 W 15th St, Suite B, Plano, TX 75075. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this site or contact us, why we collect it, and what choices you have.

We are a small local business. We are not in the data business, we do not sell personal information, and we collect the minimum we need to teach you, sell you an instrument, or fix the one you have.

2. What We Collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details — your name, email address and phone number, when you submit our contact form, call us, or sign up for lessons.
  • Lesson details — the instrument you play, your experience level, instructor preference and scheduling information.
  • Messages — anything you choose to write in the message field of our contact form.
  • Repair and purchase records — details of instruments left with us for repair, and records of sales, kept for warranty and service history.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic usage data — pages visited, approximate location (city level), browser and device type, and referring site.
  • Cookies — small files stored by your browser. See section 5.

We do not collect payment card details through this website. Payments are taken in person at the shop.

3. How We Use It

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange lessons, repairs or purchases.
  • To match you with an appropriate instructor and schedule.
  • To send you information you asked for, such as recital dates or guides you requested.
  • To keep service records for instruments we have worked on.
  • To understand which parts of this site are useful and improve them.
  • To meet our legal and tax obligations.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these situations:

  • Our instructors — your name, contact details and lesson notes are shared with the instructor teaching you, so they can prepare and reach you about scheduling.
  • Service providers — companies that host this website, deliver our email, or process our payments, acting on our instructions and bound to protect your data.
  • Legal requirements — where we are required to disclose information by law, court order, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.

5. Cookies & Analytics

This site uses a small number of cookies. Essential cookies remember your cookie preference so we do not ask you again on every page. Analytics cookies, if you accept them, help us count visits and see which pages are read.

You can decline non-essential cookies using the banner shown on your first visit, and you can clear or block cookies at any time in your browser settings. Declining will not stop you using any part of this site.

6. SMS & Email Communications

If you tick the SMS consent box on our contact form, you agree to receive text messages from Texas Music Lab about your enquiry, lesson scheduling and reminders. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. Reply HELP for assistance. Opting out of texts will not affect your lessons — we will simply contact you by phone or email instead.

If you subscribe to our occasional email updates, every message includes an unsubscribe link. We do not add people to our email list without being asked.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Texas residents, and anyone else who asks, may request that we:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is inaccurate or out of date.
  • Delete your information, where we are not required to keep it.
  • Stop sending you marketing communications.

To make any of these requests, contact us using the details in section 11. We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights. We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, or as long as tax and business records law requires.

8. Children's Privacy

We teach students under 18, and in those cases we deal with a parent or guardian. We collect only what is needed to arrange and deliver lessons. This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information online from anyone under 13 without parental involvement. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Terms of Use

Using this site

This website is provided for information about our services. You may browse and print pages for your own personal or educational use. You may not copy substantial parts of the site for commercial purposes without our permission.

Accuracy of information

We try to keep prices, availability, instructor rosters and event dates current, but they change. Nothing on this site is a binding offer. Instrument stock in particular turns over constantly — please call before travelling for something specific.

Lessons, bookings and cancellations

Lessons are booked directly with us and are not sold through this website. There is no term contract. We ask for reasonable notice if you need to cancel or pause so that we can offer the slot to someone else. Specific scheduling arrangements are agreed with you and your instructor.

Repairs

We assess instruments and quote before carrying out work, and we only do work you have authorised. Instruments left with us remain your property and are held at your risk except where loss or damage is caused by our negligence.

Third-party links

This site links to third-party sites such as Google Maps. We are not responsible for their content or their privacy practices.

Limitation of liability

This site is provided "as is". To the fullest extent permitted by Texas law, we are not liable for indirect or consequential losses arising from use of this website. Nothing here limits any liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

10. Accessibility Statement

We want this site to be usable by everyone, including people using screen readers, keyboard navigation, or magnification. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

Steps we have taken include semantic headings and landmarks, text alternatives for meaningful images, colour combinations chosen for contrast, a layout that works at 200% zoom and on small screens, and navigation that works without a mouse.

Accessibility is ongoing rather than finished. If you hit something on this site you cannot use, please tell us — it is the fastest way for us to fix it, and we will help you get what you needed in the meantime by phone or in person.

The shop itself: our Plano premises are on a single level with parking directly outside. If you have specific access requirements for a lesson or a visit, call ahead and we will make arrangements.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this policy, a request about your information, or an accessibility problem — any of these reach a person, not a queue:

  • In person or by post: Texas Music Lab, 2229 W 15th St, Suite B, Plano, TX 75075
  • By phone: 972.985.0900 (Tue–Sat 12–6 PM)
  • Online: our contact form

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.

Still Have a Question?

If anything here is unclear, ask us. We would rather explain it than have you wondering.
