How We Stock

Every Instrument on
the Wall Was Chosen

We're a small shop, which means we can't carry everything — and that turns out to be the point. Nothing goes on the wall that one of us hasn't picked up and played, and nothing stays there because a distributor made us take twelve of them.

Every instrument gets set up on our own bench before it's sold, new ones included. A $250 guitar that plays properly will keep a beginner going. The same guitar straight out of the box, with the action a mile high, is why people quit.

Set up before it sells — action, intonation and relief checked on every instrument
Play it first, always — take it to a room, plug it in, spend as long as you need
No commission pressure — nobody here earns more by selling you the dearer one
Honest about budget — we'll tell you when the cheaper option is genuinely fine

Visit Us

The Shop Is the Point

We don't sell online. Choosing an instrument is a physical decision — how it sits, how the neck feels, whether it sounds like the thing in your head. You can't do that from a photograph.

Try Before You Buy

Take anything off the wall, find a quiet corner, plug in. Nobody will hover.

Set Up Included

Every instrument we sell leaves properly set up, and we'll adjust it again free once it's settled.

Trade-Ins Welcome

Bring what you've outgrown. We'll look at it honestly and quote you on the spot.
