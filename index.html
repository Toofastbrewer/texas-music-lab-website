Our Story

A Shop Built by
Musicians, for Musicians

Texas Music Lab was built on a simple belief: everyone deserves access to great music education, quality instruments, and honest repairs. We're a real shop staffed by real musicians who care deeply about the craft.

Founded by Jack Buras and rooted in Plano since 2007, we've helped hundreds of students find their sound. Jack passed away in October 2025 — his legacy lives on in everything we do.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first visit.

Do I need to own an instrument before starting lessons?
+
No — instruments are available to use during lessons, and our staff can help you find the right one when you're ready to buy. We won't rush you into a purchase.
What age groups do you teach?
+
We teach all ages — from young beginners (typically 7+) to adults picking up an instrument for the first time. Each instructor is experienced with different age groups and we'll match accordingly.
How long are lessons and what do they cost?
+
We offer 30-minute and 60-minute private lessons, starting at approximately $35/lesson. Contact us for current exact rates and monthly bundle pricing.
What instruments do you teach?
+
Our instructors cover acoustic guitar, electric guitar, classical guitar, flamenco, ukulele, bass guitar, and upright bass — alongside music theory in multiple genres.
Can I try an instrument before buying it?
+
Absolutely. Come into the shop and play anything that interests you. Our staff will help you find the right fit — no pressure, no commission-driven sales tactics.
Do I need an appointment for a repair?
+
Walk-ins are welcome during business hours. Bring your instrument in and we'll give you an honest assessment and quote before any work is done.

Ready to Start Your Musical Journey?

Whether you're picking up a guitar for the first time or refining decades of skill, we're here for every step.
