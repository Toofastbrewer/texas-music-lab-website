Where People Started,
Where They Got To

Every one of these began the same way — a phone call from someone who wasn't sure they'd be any good at it.

Beginner → Recital

Maya, 11 — From Zero to the Spring Recital

Started with Ryan having never held a guitar. Fourteen months later she played a solo arrangement of a piece she picked herself, in front of sixty people, without notes.

14 months · Acoustic guitar with Ryan Hughes
Returner

David, 47 — Twenty Years Off, Back on Stage

Played in bands through his twenties, then stopped completely. Came in wanting "just to get the calluses back" and ended up gigging at the shop's own open mic within a year.

11 months · Electric guitar with Houston Rehmet
Switching Instruments

Priya, 29 — Guitarist Who Became a Bass Player

Came for guitar lessons, tried an upright in the shop on a whim, and never really went back. Now holds down the low end in two working bands around DFW.

2 years · Bass & upright with Cade Meaderis
Breaking a Plateau

Marcus, 34 — Ten Years Stuck, Then Not

Self-taught for a decade and completely stalled. Freddy spent the first three lessons rebuilding his right hand and the fourth explaining why nothing he'd tried had worked.

18 months · Jazz guitar & theory with Freddy Ezeala
Family

The Ortegas — Three Generations, One Slot

Grandfather, father and daughter book back-to-back on Saturday mornings. They started so the youngest wouldn't feel alone in it. All three are still going.

‍‍ 3 years · Guitar with Scott Montgomery
Late Start

Eleanor, 68 — "I Always Meant To"

Retired, decided she'd waited long enough, and booked a 30-minute slot expecting to be the oldest person in the building. She was not. She now plays every day.

8 months · Classical guitar with Ryan Hughes

Where Our Students Are Playing Now

School ensembles, worship teams, wedding bands, open mics and a couple of proper touring outfits.
