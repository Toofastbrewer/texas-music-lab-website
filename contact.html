FAQs

Before You Reach Out

Quick answers to the most common questions.

How quickly will you respond to my message?
+
We respond to all contact form submissions within one business day, usually sooner. If you need a quicker response, call us directly at 972.985.0900 during business hours.
Do I need an appointment for a repair assessment?
+
No appointment needed — walk-ins are welcome during business hours. Bring your instrument in and we'll take a look, give you an honest quote, and let you know the turnaround time before any work starts.
How soon can I start lessons after contacting you?
+
In most cases, we can get you scheduled within the same week. We'll match you with an instructor, confirm availability, and get your first lesson on the calendar as quickly as possible.
What should I bring to my first lesson?
+
If you own an instrument, bring it — playing on your own guitar or bass makes the first lesson much more productive. If you don't have one yet, no problem: we have instruments available to use and can help you find the right one to purchase.
Can I reschedule or cancel a lesson?
+
Yes. We ask for at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling so we can offer the slot to another student. Call us directly or mention it when you contact us and we'll work with you.
