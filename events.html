Never Played for People?

The First Time Is
Always the Worst One

Almost everyone who plays at our open mic was terrified the first time. It's a genuinely small room full of people who are either about to do the same thing or remember doing it.

If you'd like to try but the idea of a sign-up sheet is too much, tell your instructor. We'll put you first on the list, get it over with early, and you can spend the rest of the night enjoying it.

✓
One song is fine — nobody is counting, and short sets are welcome
✓
Backing is available — an instructor will happily play alongside you
✓
Gear is provided — amps, mics and stands are all set up and ready
✓
Mistakes don't matter — genuinely, nobody remembers them but you
